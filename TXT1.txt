Thought Experiment Imagine a very long, cylindrical solenoid coil made of copper wire. The coil is short-circuited. Drop a lightweight magnet (Magnet 1) into the upper part of the coil. Due to electromagnetic induction and Lenz's law, it experiences opposition to its downward motion. After some time, while Magnet 1 is still inside the coil and moving downward, drop a second magnet (Magnet 2) from above. Magnet 2 is stronger and heavier than Magnet 1. Both magnets are now moving inside the same short-circuited coil, so their magnetic fields and the induced current in the coil interact with each other. The Question ❓ What will happen to Magnet 1 when the stronger and heavier Magnet 2 enters the same coil from above?
