# Diode Rectification in a Solenoid Experiment

Two magnets move toward a solenoid from opposite ends while the terminals form a loop containing an ideal diode.

In a resistive short circuit, the symmetric induced voltages can oppose one another. The diode changes this behavior by allowing current during only one polarity of the induced voltage.

## Observation

The magnetic braking force becomes dependent on direction. This can make the mechanical response asymmetric even though the original geometry is symmetric.

- The diode blocks one current polarity.
- The conducting polarity produces magnetic damping.
- The result depends on the magnets' orientation and motion.
